What is an ATS (Applicant Tracking System) and why do companies use it?
An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by employers (e.g. Workday, Lever, Greenhouse, Taleo) to automatically scan, parse, and filter thousands of resumes before a human recruiter ever sees them. Over 75% of resumes are filtered out automatically if they lack the required keywords, skills, or clean formatting.
How is the ATS Match Score calculated on this tool?
Our algorithm compares technical keywords, developer tools, languages, and frameworks between your resume and the Job Description (70% weight), evaluates the presence of quantifiable metrics like numbers and percentages (15% weight), and audits active action verbs like 'Architected', 'Engineered', and 'Optimized' (15% weight) to produce a realistic 0–100% score.
Is my resume private and safe? Does it get uploaded or saved?
Yes, 100% private. Unlike commercial resume builders that store your personal contact details on their cloud servers, VASTUDA parses your resume entirely inside your browser's local memory (client-side JavaScript). Zero bytes of your resume leave your device or get sent over the network.
How can I boost my ATS score from below 60% to above 85%?
1. Look at the 'Missing Critical Skills' list generated by this tool and incorporate those technologies into your Projects or Technical Skills section. 2. Rewrite project bullets to include measurable outcomes (e.g., 'reduced API latency by 40% across 1,500 requests'). 3. Use standard section headers (Education, Skills, Experience, Projects).
Can ATS systems read fancy Canva templates or two-column graphic resumes?
Most legacy ATS parsers struggle with multi-column layouts, text boxes, and graphic skill bars, often resulting in scrambled text or missed sections. A clean, single-column text-based format is universally recognized and has the highest ATS pass rate.
Is this ATS Resume Scanner completely free for college students?
Yes! VASTUDA is completely free, open to all students, and requires no paid subscriptions, credit cards, or hidden limits.
