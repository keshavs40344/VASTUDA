What does CleanWrite Prose & Readability Sentinel do?

It analyzes written English for readability scores (Flesch-Kincaid, Coleman-Liau), detects passive voice, highlights clichés, and suggests concise alternatives.

Is my writing monitored or used for AI training?

No! CleanWrite does not transmit your text to any server or AI model. All natural language statistics run entirely inside your browser.

Who should use CleanWrite?

Students writing academic research papers, developers drafting documentation, and job seekers polishing cover letters.
