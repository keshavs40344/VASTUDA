What is a college viva voce exam and why do students find it intimidating?
A viva voce is an oral examination conducted by college professors or external university examiners during semester lab practicals and final year project defenses. Because you have to answer verbally on the spot without Google or notes, having concise, 2-sentence crisp answers is the secret to scoring full marks.
What is the 'Professor Trap Follow-Up' section in this tool?
When students recite standard textbook definitions, experienced professors and technical interviewers intentionally ask a subtle follow-up question to test if you actually understand the concept or just memorized words. This tool exposes those exact trap questions along with the winning answers.
Which core computer science subjects are covered?
We cover the 5 most heavily tested subjects in engineering syllabi and campus placement rounds: Database Management Systems (DBMS), Operating Systems (OS), Computer Networks (CN), Object-Oriented Programming (OOPs), and Machine Learning (ML).
How can I use this tool for quick revision 30 minutes before an exam?
Select the subject being tested, flip through the flashcards, click 'Mastered' for concepts you know, and 'Still Confused' for ones you need to review. The progress tracker will keep you focused on weak areas.
Why do tech companies like TCS, Infosys, Amazon, and startups ask basic CS fundamentals?
Frameworks (like React or Django) change every few years, but foundational concepts like ACID transactions, process synchronization, 3-way handshakes, and OOPs principles never become obsolete. Strong fundamentals prove you can learn any technology quickly.
