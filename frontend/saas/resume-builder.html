Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About VASTUDA Career Platform

How does the 100% Free Vector PDF download work?

Commercial resume builders charge $2.95 to $24.95 to download your PDF after spending hours writing. VASTUDA uses standard browser print media isolation (`@media print`) that renders true vector-sharp text directly onto A4 dimensions. This text remains completely selectable and readable by ATS scanning bots with zero watermarks and zero fees.

How many templates are available and how do I choose?

We provide 60+ dynamic combinations across 6 structural categories (ATS Classic, Modern Tech, Silicon Valley Two-Column, Creative Portfolio, Executive Leadership, and Academic Multi-Page CV). Each category has multiple visual styles, 12 curated palettes, and a custom color picker.

Should I include a photo on my resume?

In the US, UK, and Canada, hiring standards strongly discourage photos to prevent unconscious bias. However, in India, continental Europe (Germany, France), and the Middle East, professional headshots are often expected. VASTUDA gives you full control: use the 1-click Photo Toggle in the top toolbar to switch photo rendering ON or OFF instantly without losing your photo.

Is my personal data or resume uploaded to any server?

No. We operate under strict zero-cloud-egress architecture. All personal details, bullet edits, and PDF generation occur in your browser's local RAM and LocalStorage. When you want to save a permanent backup, use the Backup (Save JSON) button to download your master profile directly onto your laptop.
