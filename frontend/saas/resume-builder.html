Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Resume Guide

Expert advice on ATS compatibility, 1-page formatting, and student placement tips

Is this online resume builder truly 100% free without hidden paywalls?
Yes! Commercial platforms like ResumeBuilder.com or Zety force users to spend 30 minutes filling out information only to demand credit cards at the final download step. VASTUDA provides 100% free vector-sharp PDF downloads with no watermarks, no subscriptions, and zero login barriers.
Are these resume templates applicant tracking system (ATS) compliant?
Yes. All 4 templates are engineered following strict ATS standards: clean hierarchy, standard section headers, machine-readable text fonts, and zero graphic charts that break OCR parsers.
How can fresh engineering students with no prior work experience stand out?
Put your Technical Projects front and center! Recruiters hire freshers based on their project depth and GitHub contributions. Write bullet points with concrete numbers (e.g., 'Built an image classification model in PyTorch achieving 94% accuracy' or 'Implemented REST API in FastAPI serving 500+ requests/min').
How do I keep my resume to a strict 1-page length?
Select the 'Silicon Valley 2-Column' or 'Classic Minimal' template. Limit your bullet points to 2–3 impactful lines per project, group your skills into concise comma-separated lists, and remove high school details if you are already in college.
How can I save and continue editing my resume later?
Click the 'Save JSON' button in the top bar. This downloads a tiny .json file to your device. Next time you visit VASTUDA, simply click 'Import' and select that file to restore your entire resume immediately.
Is my resume data stored on any database or cloud server?
Zero cloud storage. All inputs, previews, and PDF generation take place locally within your browser's memory. Your personal phone number, email, and identity are 100% private to your device.
