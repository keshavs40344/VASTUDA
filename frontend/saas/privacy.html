Staunt Browser Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 • Product Version: 2.0.0
Zero Personal Telemetry Architecture

1. Fundamental Privacy Philosophy

Staunt Browser is engineered on sovereign privacy principles. We believe that your browsing activity belongs exclusively to you. Staunt Browser does not track, collect, store, sell, or profile your personal data or browsing behavior.

Unlike commercial advertising browsers, Staunt Browser operates without tracking identifiers, marketing SDKs, diagnostic telemetry pingbacks, or background behavioral analytics.

2. Data Collection and Processing

Staunt Browser processes the minimal technical data required to render web content and store user preferences locally on your device:

  • Browsing History & Bookmarks: Saved locally on your device using local storage or on-device SQLite databases. This data is never sent to our servers.
  • Downloaded Files: Initiated directly by user request and saved solely to your device's designated local Downloads folder.
  • Form Autofill & Passwords: Handled locally on your device with client-side zero-knowledge encryption where configured.
  • Cookies & Site Data: Retained on your device according to standard web platform specifications and can be cleared at any time via Settings.

3. Platform-Specific Implementations

Android Edition (Package: com.staunt.browser)

The Android edition utilizes the system-provided Android WebView runtime. Network traffic passes directly between your device and the websites you visit.

  • Network-Layer Filter Engine: Uses a local curated ad and tracker blocklist (EasyList format) bundled in the application package to intercept advertising and tracking domains directly inside WebView without external lookup servers.
  • Permissions: Requested permissions (Internet, Network State, Downloads, Camera/Audio for optional WebRTC video calls, Location for requested web geolocations) require explicit runtime user consent and are used exclusively when interacting with web pages.
  • Local SQLite Storage: History and Bookmarks are written to local private SQLite storage inside the application's sandboxed directory.

Windows Desktop Edition

The Windows edition runs as a standalone 64-bit desktop application with native process isolation.

  • All browsing state, session storage, and bookmarks are kept in the user's local profile directory (%APPDATA%).
  • No remote crash reporters or diagnostic telemetry services are activated.

Web Edition

The Web application edition runs inside modern web browsers.

  • Client state is preserved in the browser's origin localStorage and IndexedDB.
  • Cross-origin navigation may utilize an in-memory reverse-proxy gateway to overcome frame embedding restrictions without persisting traffic logs.

4. Third-Party Websites and Services

When you navigate to third-party websites or use external search engines (such as Google, DuckDuckGo, or Bing), those third parties receive your requests in accordance with their respective privacy policies and terms of service. Staunt Browser transmits standard HTTP headers (such as User-Agent) necessary for web compatibility and enables Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where supported.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Staunt Browser does not transmit your personal data to any central server, you maintain complete custody of your information. You can delete your history, cookies, cache, and stored bookmarks at any time:

  • Open Settings → Storage & Site Data
  • Click Clear Browser Cache, Clear Site Data & Cookies, or Clear All Browsing Data
